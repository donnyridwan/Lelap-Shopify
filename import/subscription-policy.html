This policy explains how Lelap subscriptions work. By starting a subscription you agree to these terms.

How your subscription works

When you choose “Subscribe & save”, we send a new supply of your formula every month and charge the payment method you used at checkout on each delivery. Subscription orders get 20% off the one-time price, and shipping is free.

Every new plan is reviewed by a licensed doctor before your first order ships. If the doctor decides the formula isn’t right for you, we cancel the subscription and refund you in full.

Billing date

Your billing date is the day of your first order, repeated every month. We’ll email you a reminder before each renewal.

Skip, change or pause

You can skip a delivery, change your formula or pause your subscription at any time before your next billing date from your account, or by messaging us on WhatsApp.

Cancel anytime

There’s no minimum commitment. Cancel from your account or by contacting us before your next billing date and you won’t be charged again. Orders that have already been charged and shipped can’t be cancelled, but they’re covered by our 30-Night Sleep Promise.

30-Night Sleep Promise

Give Lelap 30 nights. If you’re not sleeping better, we’ll refund your first order. No need to send anything back. See our refund policy for details.

Price changes

If the price of your subscription changes, we’ll tell you by email at least 14 days before the new price applies, so you can cancel if you don’t want to continue.

Failed payments

If a payment fails, we’ll try again over the next few days and let you know. If we still can’t charge you, your subscription will be paused until you update your payment details.

Questions

Message us on WhatsApp or use the Help page and our care team will get back to you.
